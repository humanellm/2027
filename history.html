Workshop History

HUMANE-LLM is the first edition of this workshop, building on related LLM4Good experience at ACM UMAP.

First edition

HUMANE-LLM is the first edition of a workshop focused specifically on human-centered and responsible LLMs for agency-preserving Intelligent User Interfaces.

Related workshops

Two editions of a similar workshop with a greater focus on personalization were carried out at the ACM International Conference on User Modeling, Adaptation and Personalization (UMAP). Accepted contributions covered applications of LLMs for education, career guidance, health, storytelling, sustainability, disaster response, and cybersecurity.

Related workshop website

Planned outcomes

  • A dedicated workshop website and a distributed CFP shared through major mailing lists, social media, and professional networks related to IUI.
  • Accepted and presented workshop papers published in the CEUR joint proceedings volume, subject to final arrangements.
  • A journal publication co-authored by the workshop organizers and interested participants discussing aspects related to HUMANE.
  • Follow-up workshops at IUI and other related conferences, if applicable.
