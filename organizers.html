Workshop Organizers

Main contact

Noemi Mauro

University of Torino, Italy

Tenure-track Assistant Professor at the University of Torino. Her research interests concern user modelling, recommender systems, information filtering, and information visualization. She has previously organized related workshops at UMAP and RecSys, including the International Workshop on User Modeling, Personalization, and Adaptive Systems for Sustainability and Social Good.

Email · Website

Julia Neidhardt

CDL-RecSys, TU Wien, Austria

Tenure-track Assistant Professor at TU Wien with a background in mathematics and computer science. She leads the Christian Doppler Laboratory on Recommender Systems and is UNESCO Co-Chair for Digital Humanism. She has successfully organized similar workshops, including LLM4Good at UMAP.

Email · Website

Yashar Deldjoo

Polytechnic University of Bari, Italy

Associate Professor of Computer Science at DEI, Poliba. His research focuses on trustworthy recommender systems, generative and agentic recommendations, and the evaluation of LLM-based systems. He has organized workshops and delivered tutorials at KDD, RecSys, SIGIR, CIKM, WSDM, ECIR, ECAI, and IJCAI-ECAI.

Email · Website

Ludovico Boratto

University of Cagliari, Italy

Associate Professor of Computer Science. His research focuses on recommender systems and their impact on different stakeholders, considering both accuracy and beyond-accuracy evaluation metrics. He has organized workshops at RecSys, SIGIR, ECIR, CHItaly, ECML-PKDD, CIKM, ICDM, and EICS, and delivered tutorials at CIKM, UMAP, RecSys, ICDE, ECIR, WSDM, ICDM, ECAI, and DSAA.

Email · Website

Dominik Kowald

University of Graz and Know Center Research GmbH, Austria

Professor of AI-based information retrieval in digital humanities at the University of Graz. He is also head of the FAIR-AI research area at Know Center Graz, a research center for trustworthy AI in Austria, and has experience organizing related workshops such as Hybrid AI for Human-Centric Personalization.
